# 🇬🇧 Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy explains how the SkyDodge mobile application ("**SkyDodge**", the "**App**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") handles information when you use it. It also sets out the limits of our responsibility. Please read it carefully. By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

**Data controller:** Marien Fournier, sole developer ("the Developer").
**Contact:** marienfournier11@icloud.com
**App:** SkyDodge, distributed on the Apple App Store.

## 1. Summary (plain language)

SkyDodge is a casual arcade game. **We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other directly identifying personal information.** The App stores your game progress **locally on your device**. The only data that leaves your device is limited, non-identifying advertising, analytics and diagnostic data processed by the third-party services listed in Section 4, and any score you choose to submit to Apple Game Center.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Stored only on your device
- Game progress: scores, coins, diamonds, unlocked planes/themes/trails, daily-challenge progress, settings and preferences. This is saved in a local file in the App's storage and is **not transmitted to any server operated by the Developer** (the Developer operates no server).

### 2.2 Collected automatically by third-party SDKs
- **Advertising identifiers and advertising data** (e.g., a device advertising identifier / IDFA where permitted, ad impressions and interactions) — used by Google AdMob to display ads.
- **Aggregated usage / product-interaction data** (in-app events such as runs started/ended, screens opened, anonymous app-instance identifier) — used by Firebase Analytics.
- **Diagnostic data** (crash reports, performance and device technical data) — used by Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs and improve stability.

All analytics events are aggregated and non-identifying scalars (scores, counters, enumerated values). We do not attach your identity, free text, contacts, or location to these events.

### 2.3 Motion data (gyroscope)
If you enable the optional **tilt control**, the App reads device motion data (gyroscope/accelerometer via Apple CoreMotion) **solely to move the plane in real time. This motion data is processed entirely on your device, is never stored, and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.**

### 2.4 Information we do NOT collect
- No name, email, phone number, or contact details.
- No precise or coarse geolocation.
- No access to photos, microphone, camera, contacts, or health/biometric data.
- No account creation and no user-generated text.

You may also purchase the optional **"Remove Ads"** in-app purchase to remove all advertising.

## 6. International Data Transfers
